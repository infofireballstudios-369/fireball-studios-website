ABOUT US

3D Visualisation & Engineering Studio in London

A London studio combining engineering precision with premium 3D visualisation — architectural, automotive, product and CFD projects across the UK and Europe.

Fireball Studios is a London-based 3D visualisation and engineering design studio with a Masters-level background in mechanical engineering and 15 years of combined 3D visualisation experience. We deliver complete end-to-end solutions for property, architecture, automotive, product and engineering projects — from CAD modelling and design refinement through to photorealistic renders, interactive 3D experiences and technical documentation.

Complete End-to-End Solutions

OUR STORY

Every great project starts with an idea. It may begin as a sketch, a CAD model, a floor plan, a concept in your head or simply a problem that needs solving. Fireball Studios turns that idea into something you can see, understand, develop and share.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

About Fireball Studios

Common questions about who we are and how we work.

What is Fireball Studios?

Fireball Studios is a London-based 3D visualisation and engineering design studio providing architectural, automotive and product visualisation, CAD and 3D modelling, engineering analysis (FEA and CFD) and technical documentation for clients across the UK and Europe.

Where is Fireball Studios based?

We are based in London, UK. All work is delivered remotely, with regular checkpoints over email, video call or in-person meetings for London-based clients.

What makes Fireball Studios different from other visualisation studios?

Two things. First, an engineering background — our team holds a Masters degree in mechanical engineering, so we understand the technical side of what we visualise, not just the aesthetics. Second, we deliver complete end-to-end solutions: CAD modelling, engineering analysis, visualisation, interactive 3D and documentation under one roof, with no handoffs to third parties.

How much experience does Fireball Studios have?

Our team brings over 15 years of combined experience in 3D visualisation, CAD modelling and engineering design, working across property, architecture, automotive, product and research projects.

What types of clients do you work with?

Property developers, architects, interior designers, estate agents, automotive brands and tuners, product designers, manufacturers, engineering firms, university research teams, homeowners and private clients.

Do you offer a complete solution or individual services?

Both. Some clients come to us for a single deliverable — a render, a CAD model, a CFD study. Others need a full end-to-end package from concept through to final documentation. We scope each project individually and quote transparently.

Email info@fireballstudios.co.uk or use the enquiry form on our contact page. Send us a brief, any source files you have, and your target timeline. We'll come back with a recommended approach and quote.
