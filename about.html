ABOUT US

Bringing Ideas to Life, Virtually

A London studio combining creative visualisation with technical understanding.

BRINGING IDEAS TO LIFE, VIRTUALLY

Ideas made easier to see, understand and build

Every great project starts with an idea. It may begin as a sketch, a CAD model, a floor plan, a concept in your head or simply a problem that needs solving. Fireball Studios turns that idea into something you can see, understand, develop and share.

We combine 3D visualisation, CAD, 3D modelling, design optimisation and technical documentation to support property, architecture, engineering and product development projects, from the first concept through to design refinement, technical development and final presentation.

OUR PHILOSOPHY

Good design should be easy to communicate

Whether you are presenting a property, developing a product, refining an architectural concept or solving a technical challenge, the right visual and technical information can make all the difference. We use technology to simplify the design process and help each project move forward with clarity.

OUR APPROACH

We work with you

Every project is different. We begin by understanding your idea, objectives and constraints, then choose the most practical combination of visualisation, modelling, design support and documentation. Clear communication and dependable delivery guide the work from first discussion to final handover.

OUR STRENGTH

Design thinking meets technical understanding

Strong visualisation must do more than look impressive. Dimensions need to work, materials need to behave realistically, spaces need to feel believable and technical information needs to be clear. We combine creative visualisation with technical thinking to produce work that remains meaningful throughout the design process.

OUR COMMITMENT

Quality without unnecessary complexity

Every project has a budget, a deadline and a purpose. We focus on what is actually needed, then use the right tools and processes to deliver it efficiently. Our aim is professional work, sensible pricing and practical value without compromising care or clarity.
