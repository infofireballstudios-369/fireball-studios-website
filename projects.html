ENGINEERING · CFD 04

Cross Twisted Tape Heat Exchanger Study

A Cross Twisted Tape with V cuts was designed to generate swirl, increase turbulence and reduce thermal boundary layer resistance. ANSYS Fluent simulations and water tests from Reynolds numbers 500 to 2,000 showed up to a 50 per cent improvement in Nusselt number.
