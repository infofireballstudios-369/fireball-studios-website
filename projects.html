AUTOMOTIVE 05

Interactive Car Configurator for a Custom Tuner

A custom car tuner approached Fireball Studios to build a real-time 3D configurator that would let their customers explore body kits, paint finishes and wheel options before placing an order. The result was a branded, browser-based configurator that gave the tuner a clear point of differentiation against competitors.

The brief

Develop a custom online configurator for the tuner's range of car accessories — something customers could interact with directly on the website.

Our approach

Modelled the vehicle and accessory range in Blender and 3ds Max, then built a real-time three.js configurator optimised for browser performance.

What we delivered

A fully branded, interactive 3D configurator with real-time material and colour changes, embedded on the tuner's website.

The result

The tuner now presents a configurable proposal to end customers — differentiating their offering from standard accessory catalogues.

REAL ESTATE 06

Interactive Property Configurator for a London Estate Agent

A London property agent needed a way to present homes to buyers without relying on a single fixed render. Fireball Studios built an interactive property configurator that lets clients switch between roof types, wall colours and day/night lighting — helping buyers make faster, more confident decisions.

The brief

Build a configurable property viewer where buyers could change roof type and wall colours, and preview the property in day and night settings.

Our approach

Modelled the house in Blender and 3ds Max, then developed a three.js-powered configurator that renders in any modern browser — no plugins, no downloads.

What we delivered

A branded, browser-based configurator with a day/night toggle and switchable roof and wall materials.

The result

The agent can show multiple property variations in a single meeting, reducing buyer decision fatigue and speeding up the sales conversation.

ENGINEERING · CFD 08

Cross Twisted Tape Heat Exchanger — CFD Study

A scientific research project required analysis of the temperature gradient in a cylindrical pipe fitted with different internal inserts. Using SolidWorks for CAD and ANSYS Fluent for CFD simulation, Fireball Studios produced temperature gradient data at multiple cross-sections along the pipe length.

The brief

Check the temperature gradient of fluid flow in a cylindrical pipe with different internal inserts.

Our approach

Modelled the geometry in SolidWorks, ran CFD simulations in ANSYS Fluent across Reynolds numbers 500–2,000, and extracted temperature data at multiple cross-sections.

What we delivered

Temperature gradient data at different cross-sections of the pipe along its length, presented as a structured technical study.

The result

The cross twisted tape design produced better fluid mixing at higher Reynolds numbers, driving turbulent flow and improving heat transfer efficiency by up to 50% in Nusselt number.
