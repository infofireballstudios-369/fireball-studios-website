WHAT WE OFFER

3D Visualisation, CAD &
Engineering Services in London

From architectural and automotive visualisation to CAD modelling and engineering analysis — one studio, from concept to completion.

Fireball Studios is a London-based 3D visualisation and engineering design studio. We provide 3D architectural and automotive visualisation, CAD and 3D modelling, engineering analysis, product visualisation, prototyping support and professional technical documentation. Our clients include property developers, architects, interior designers, automotive brands, manufacturers and engineering firms across the UK and Europe.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Service questions

Common questions about how we work with clients on visualisation, CAD and engineering projects.

Do you offer both visualisation and engineering services?

Yes. Fireball Studios brings visualisation and engineering under one roof. That means a single project can move from CAD modelling and engineering analysis through to photorealistic rendering and interactive 3D — without handing off between studios.

Can you handle a project that needs visualisation, CAD and analysis?

Yes. Many of our projects combine several services — for example, a product concept needs CAD modelling, FEA validation, and a photorealistic render for marketing. We structure these as a single engagement with clear deliverables and a single point of contact.

How do I know which service I need?

If you're unsure, send us your goals and any source material you have (drawings, sketches, CAD files, reference images). We'll recommend the right mix of services and explain what each stage delivers. There's no obligation — the initial conversation is free.

Do you work on small projects or only large developments?

Both. We work on everything from a single product render or a small CAD modelling task through to full property developments with multiple visualisation outputs. Pricing scales with scope, so small projects aren't penalised.

How are your services priced?

Pricing depends on scope, complexity, the number of outputs and the delivery timeline. Most projects are quoted as a fixed price after we've reviewed the brief and source files. Send your brief to info@fireballstudios.co.uk for a clear quote.

What's the typical delivery timeline?

Small still renders or CAD tasks can be delivered in a few days. Larger visualisation projects typically take 1–3 weeks. Interactive 3D experiences, animations and combined engineering projects usually take 3–6 weeks. We confirm the schedule before work begins.

Not sure which service fits?

Send us your brief and source files. We'll come back with a recommended approach, timeline and quote.
