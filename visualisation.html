3D Architectural Visualisation
in London

Photorealistic renders, walkthrough animations and interactive 3D tours for property developers, architects and interior designers.

3D architectural visualisation helps property developers, architects and designers communicate how a building, interior or development will look before it is built. Fireball Studios creates photorealistic renders, walkthrough animations and interactive 3D tours for residential, commercial and mixed-use projects across London, the UK and Europe.

What we deliver

Whether you're marketing a new development, presenting a design to planning, or helping buyers explore a property remotely, we produce visuals that are accurate, on-brand and built to communicate clearly.

  • Photorealistic interior & exterior renders
  • Architectural walkthrough animations
  • Interactive 3D tours and configurators
  • Day / night lighting and material studies
  • Aerial and site context visualisations
  • Marketing and presentation visuals

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

3D visualisation questions

Common questions about working with Fireball Studios on a visualisation project.

What source files do you need to start?

Floor plans, elevations, sections, CAD or BIM files (Revit, SketchUp, Rhino, ArchiCAD), reference images and material specifications. If you only have sketches, we can work from those — we'll advise what additional information is needed as the project progresses.

How long does an architectural visualisation take?

Typical timelines are 1–2 weeks for a small set of still renders and 3–6 weeks for animations or interactive 3D tours. Timescales depend on the complexity of the design, the quality of source files and the number of review rounds. We confirm a delivery schedule before work begins.

Can you produce planning submission visuals?

Yes. We regularly produce visuals for planning applications, design review panels and public consultations. We can match local authority requirements on image size, viewpoint context and site lines.

Do you provide interactive 3D tours?

Yes. Interactive tours let clients, buyers or stakeholders explore a property in a web browser — with configurable finishes, day/night lighting and walkthrough paths. See the Courtyard House project for an example.

Do you work with clients outside London?

Yes. We're based in London and work with clients across the UK and Europe. All reviews and delivery happen remotely, with regular checkpoints over email or video call.

How much does 3D visualisation cost?

Costs depend on scope — the number of images, complexity, level of detail and delivery format (still, animation or interactive). Send a brief with your goals and source files to info@fireballstudios.co.uk and we'll return a clear quote.
