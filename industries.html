FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Industry questions

Common questions about the sectors we work in and how we adapt to each one.

What industries does Fireball Studios work with?

We work across four broad sectors: property, architecture and real estate; transport and mobility (automotive, aerospace, robotics); engineering and manufacturing (industrial equipment, manufacturing, medical devices); and product and innovation (consumer products, R&D, startups). Our services are industry-agnostic — if a project involves CAD, 3D visualisation or engineering analysis, we can help.

Do you specialise in a particular industry?

We have the deepest experience in property visualisation and automotive, but our service mix — CAD modelling, 3D visualisation and engineering analysis — applies to almost any physical product or building. Our CFD heat exchanger study and glass sculpture case studies both sit outside our 'core' property and automotive sectors and demonstrate that range.

Do you work with international clients?

Yes. We're based in London and work with clients across the UK and Europe. All reviews and delivery happen remotely — via email and video call — so time zone differences are manageable. For projects requiring in-person meetings, we can travel within the UK and EU.

Can you handle projects in multiple industries at once?

Yes. Some clients span several sectors — for example, an industrial equipment manufacturer producing both consumer-facing and B2B products. The underlying skills are the same: CAD, modelling, visualisation and engineering analysis. We adapt the deliverables to suit each audience.

Do you work with startups and small businesses?

Yes. We work with early-stage startups through to large established organisations. Small projects aren't penalised on pricing — our fees scale with scope, and we're happy to discuss the most cost-effective path for an initial MVP or concept validation.

How do I know if my project fits?

If your project involves a physical product, building, vehicle or engineered component — and needs either visual output (renders, animations, interactive 3D) or technical output (CAD, FEA, CFD, documentation) — it's likely a good fit. Send us a brief and we'll confirm within a working day.

Don't see your industry?

Our services aren't industry-specific. If your project involves CAD, 3D visualisation or engineering analysis, we'd like to hear about it.
