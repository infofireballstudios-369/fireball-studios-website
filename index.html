Step inside an interactive courtyard house designed to show how architectural ideas can be explored before construction begins. Rotate the view, compare daylight and night settings, and examine the relationship between the living spaces, landscaped courtyard, swimming pool and surrounding garden. The model presents a modern home with considered circulation, practical room planning and a strong connection between indoors and outdoors. This real estate visualisation gives developers, architects, agents and buyers in London a clearer, more engaging way to review a proposed home.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Planning a visualisation or engineering project?

Clear answers about our services, process and project requirements.

What services does Fireball Studios provide?

We provide 3D architectural and real estate visualisation, CAD and 3D modelling, mechanical design, FEA, CFD and thermal analysis, design optimisation, product visualisation, prototyping support and technical documentation.

Where is Fireball Studios based?

We are based in London, UK, and work remotely with clients across the UK and Europe.

Who do you work with?

We support architects, interior designers, property developers, estate agents, construction companies, product designers, manufacturers, engineering firms, homeowners and private clients.

Can you create interactive 3D property visualisations?

Yes. We create interactive property experiences, walkthroughs and photorealistic renders that help teams and buyers explore a design before construction begins.

What information do you need to start a 3D visualisation?

Useful starting material includes floor plans, elevations, sketches, CAD or BIM files, reference images, material choices and details of the intended audience. We can discuss alternatives if your concept is still at an early stage.

Can you work from sketches or incomplete CAD files?

Yes. We can develop early ideas into accurate 3D models and help identify the additional information needed for visualisation, design review or technical development.

Do you provide CFD and thermal analysis?

Yes. Our CFD work can assess fluid flow, pressure, velocity, heat transfer and thermal behaviour, with clear outputs and technical documentation suited to the agreed project scope.

What is included in a project quotation?

A quotation is based on the scope, available source information, required outputs, complexity, timescale and review requirements. We confirm deliverables and assumptions before work begins.

How long does a project take?

Timescales depend on complexity, the quality of source files, the number of outputs and the review process. Once we understand the brief, we provide a practical delivery estimate.

How can I start a project?

Send your brief through our enquiry form or email info.fireballstudios@gmail.com. Include your goals, desired outputs, target date and any drawings or reference material you can share.
